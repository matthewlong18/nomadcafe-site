THE APRON
One moss-green bib apron with a dashed stitched edge: a narrow bib over the chest, a neck strap, waist ties at the bottom of the ribcage, and a wider skirt over the belly and thighs. It is fabric, so it follows the body's shape and pose.

PRINT LAYOUT (all cream, flat screen-printed onto the fabric)
1. MOCHA: small serif capitals in the top-left corner of the bib (as the viewer sees it), just under the neck strap. Its width is about one third of the bib's width.
2. BADGE: exactly one round line-art badge (a circle containing a sun rising over wavy dunes, with three small four-point stars). Centred left-to-right on the apron, on the belly. Its top edge sits just below the waistline where the waist ties attach.
3. NOMAD CAFÉ: one line of serif capitals, centred directly under the badge with a small gap (about a tenth of the badge's diameter). About as wide as the badge, or slightly wider.

CONSISTENT SIZE
Size the print against the sloth itself so it matches every other drawing:
- Badge diameter = about three quarters of the width of the sloth's head.
- MOCHA letters are small, about the height of the sloth's eye markings.
- NOMAD CAFÉ letters are about the same height as MOCHA's letters, or slightly larger.

REALISTIC PLACEMENT
- The print sits on the fabric and bends with it: foreshortened if the body is turned, curved slightly over the belly, rotated with the body if lying down.
- Arms, hands, cups, coffee pots or counters in front of the apron cover parts of the print, exactly as they would in real life. Do not move anything to reveal it, and do not shift the print to avoid them.
- It never floats on top of an arm or object.

RULES
Exactly one MOCHA, one badge and one NOMAD CAFÉ. Nothing else printed on the apron. Spell exactly: MOCHA, NOMAD CAFÉ.
